About This Role

We are seeking an experienced and compassionate Senior Nurse Practitioner to join our growing Cardiology Department. In this role, you will collaborate with a multidisciplinary team to deliver exceptional patient care, mentor junior nursing staff, and contribute to quality improvement initiatives across the department.

The ideal candidate brings clinical expertise, a patient-first approach, and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced hospital environment. You will manage complex patient cases, perform advanced clinical assessments, and develop individualized care plans in alignment with department protocols.

This position offers significant growth opportunities, including leadership responsibilities and involvement in research and education programs at EduVerse Hospital.
